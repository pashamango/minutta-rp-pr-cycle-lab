# PRD-18 insight: deadline and publication gates

For public regulatory comments, “prepared” is not “submitted,” and “submitted”
is not “published.” The workflow must treat deadline status, signatory approval,
public-data disclosure, artifact availability, receipt capture, and publication
as separate gates. Once the deadline has elapsed, an autonomous agent must stop
at a documented blocker and request a human decision on any late-filing path;
it must not silently reuse draft artifacts or submit on the signatory's behalf.
