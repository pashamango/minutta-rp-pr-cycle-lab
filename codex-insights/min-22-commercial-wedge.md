# MIN-22 insight: sell the evidence package, not the ontology claim

The credible commercial wedge is a bounded formal-assurance artifact for one operational invariant: model, assumptions, Lean proof, countermodel, provenance, and independent replay. The ontology is useful as research context, but it is not itself a buyer outcome and must not be marketed as proof of physical reality, universal agent safety, certification, traction, or measured value. Demand remains unobserved until a real target buyer supplies a rule and requests or funds a scoped pilot.
