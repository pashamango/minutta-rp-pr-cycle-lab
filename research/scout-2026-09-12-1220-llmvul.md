## Finding

**LLMVul** (arXiv:2609.10945, submitted 2026-09-10) is a new vulnerability-labeled dataset of LLM-generated C/C++ functions mined from real production repositories. The authors report 21,430 unique functions from 226 repositories, with 1,540 ensemble-labeled vulnerable functions across 17 CWE categories. Provenance includes repository/commit/function and AI-tool metadata; manual annotation reports Cohen's kappa 0.79.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10945
Dataset DOI reported by authors: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22668216
