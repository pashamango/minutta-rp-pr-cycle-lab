## Finding
The U.S. Senate market-structure bill moved materially today: Reuters reports a revised CLARITY Act text with a large package of negotiated changes, including stronger public-official crypto ethics restrictions and possible state-attorney-general enforcement, ahead of a procedural vote expected 2026-09-15. This is a real regulatory signal for Open Money / agent-payment experiments, but it is not yet a stable enough rule set to change architecture or launch assumptions.

## Decision relevance
For Mindrock/Open Money, the important signal is not 'crypto is now safe to issue' but that U.S. token classification and distribution rules may become more explicit. If enacted in roughly this form, that could reduce legal-design uncertainty for experiments that separate utility/claims/receipts from investment contracts. However, the text is still politically contested and the near-term vote can fail or materially amend it.
